Entry Info

  


  

    

      

        Forms
        
          aspīe  n.  Also espie.

        
      

      

        Etymology
        
          AF, & CF espie.

        
      

    

  




  

    
Definitions (Senses and Subsenses)


    




      




    

      
 Note: Cp. spie.


    







      






  

    
1.

      
Stealthy investigation, observation, or watching; scouting, spying; also, information obtained by scouting; maken or setten aspie(s, to scout or spy; -- often pl., rendering L insidiae.

        Hide 13 Quotations

  • c1325(c1300) Glo.Chron.A (Clg A.11)11549

2.

      
A stealthy, treacherous plan, a plot; also, treachery; laien or setten aspies, to lay an ambush, waylay, set a trap or lay a snare (esp. of the devil).

: Erodias forsothe leide aspies [vr. settide tresoun; L insidiabatur] to him, and wolde sle him. 

    • 
              
  • (c1384) WBible(1) (Dc 369(2))Deeds 9.24
: Jewis maden a counceil, that thei schulden slee him. Forsothe the aspies [L insidiae] of hem ben maad knowun to Saul.
